Ibhotwe Food Ordering Platform

Group: Vireon5

Project Overview

Ibhotwe is a campus-based food ordering and cafeteria services platform designed to address the problem of long queues and inefficient food ordering experienced by students at university cafeterias and food vendors.

The system allows students to view available food, place orders, and choose between pickup or delivery. Vendors use the platform to manage menus and process incoming orders.

Key Features

  • Student authentication and profile management
  • Digital food catalogue with search and filter
  • Shopping cart and checkout system
  • Pickup and delivery options
  • Real-time order status tracking
  • Vendor menu management
  • Notification system
  • Administrator dashboard

Proposed Automated Process

  1. Student logs in
  2. Browses the food catalogue
  3. Selects food items
  4. Adds items to cart
  5. Proceeds to checkout
  6. Chooses pickup or delivery
  7. Vendor receives the order
  8. Vendor accepts and prepares the order
  9. Vendor updates order status
  10. Student receives notification
  11. Student collects or receives delivery
